# Sources & verification — Prop Rulebook

Verified: **25 Nov 2026**

This page is a personal study summary, not an official rulebook. Every rule card links to the official source listed here. Prop-firm rules change without much notice; re-check the source before sizing a trade. Date-dependent and legacy/limited rules are marked in the app.

## Where the rules live

- **Legacy**: rules are archived for discontinued models. Existing accounts keep them, but new accounts cannot buy.
- **Limited**: limited-time plans or account caps. The limit or the current page can conflict; read the checkout terms.
- **Date-dependent**: the rule changed on a known date. Check your purchase or account date.

Rules are grouped into account killers, payout blockers, passing conditions and reference. Each card shows the exact calculation, a dollar example, the source and the date checked.
